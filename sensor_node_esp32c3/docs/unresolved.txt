Final validation items (not unresolved design decisions)

- GPIO2 MFRC522 MOSI strapping behavior with the required external 10 kOhm pull-up.
- GPIO8/GPIO9 I2C strapping behavior with the external I2C pull-ups.
- GPIO11 VDD_SPI/module-dependent suitability for wind-pulse and MAX31865 CS.
- GPIO3 ADC/SPI-CS/OneWire electrical arbitration and the accepted OneWire pull-up leakage.
- Placeholder I2C addresses and hardware-specific driver protocols.
- Binary size headroom with app0/app1 at 0x190000.
- Native/target build and HIL validation.

Final design decisions are not unresolved: profile selection is runtime NVS + WebUI,
profile rosters are immutable at 6/12/8/9 sensors, and the final pin map in
PIN-MAPPING.md is authoritative.
